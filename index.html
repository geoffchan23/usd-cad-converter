How this is calculated

US-dollar purchases are converted at the Visa network rate, which runs about 0.3% above the market rate. That matched a real charge (US$15.60 → C$22.06), so no extra foreign transaction fee is added by default. If your card does add one, enter it under RBC FX fee. The market rate is loaded live and refreshed daily.

Your exact rate is set on the day the charge posts, not the day you buy, so treat this as a close estimate. Check your card's terms if you're unsure of its fee. Some cards charge 0%.
