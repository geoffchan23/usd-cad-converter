How this is calculated

RBC Visa cards convert US-dollar purchases at the Visa network rate and then add a foreign transaction fee (2.5% on most RBC credit cards). The market rate is loaded live and refreshed daily. Visa's own published rate is usually within a few tenths of a percent of it.

Your exact rate is set on the day the charge posts, not the day you buy, so treat this as a close estimate. Check your card's terms if you're unsure of its fee. Some cards charge 0%.
